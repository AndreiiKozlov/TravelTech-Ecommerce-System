# Travel-Tech
Mixvel - E-commerce platform for the travel industry.

As a Business Systems Analyst, I was responsible for:

• Conducted product and business analysis to identify customer needs, product gaps, growth opportunities, and translate them into actionable product initiatives.
• Prioritized product initiatives based on business value, customer impact, strategic goals, technical feasibility, risks, and time-to-market; managed and structured the product backlog.
• Analyzed end-to-end customer journeys and user scenarios, identifying UX friction points and opportunities to improve customer experience and product performance.
• Designed business logic and product architecture for a high-load flight booking and payment platform within a GDS ecosystem, including complex transactions, state management, versioning, and failure scenarios.
• Integrated with Sirena Travel (GDS), SLtech (insurance), Uniteller (payment gateway), and Leonardo (airlines; EDIFACT, AIRIMP protocols).
• Integrated with a pricing service utilizing ATPCO fare data, supporting complex airline pricing and fare-management scenarios.
• Defined integration solutions within a microservices architecture using REST, Kafka, and RabbitMQ, connecting internal services with external travel providers.
• Independently authored product and system documentation, including BRD, FRD, SRS, API specifications, Use Cases, and User Stories.
• Used SQL and Cypher for data analysis and data-driven product decisions; independently validated APIs and product functionality using Postman, Swagger, and Insomnia.
• A/B tested.
• Bridged business, product, and engineering teams by translating strategic objectives into product requirements, epics, releases, and delivery plans.
